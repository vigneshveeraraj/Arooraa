# How AROORAA Works

## The journey

Discovery → Product Strategy → UX → Architecture → Engineering → AI → Cloud → Launch →
Continuous Support.

Engagements can start at any stage — the goal is not to force every client through the same
package, but to solve the current problem and build a reliable path forward. A client may enter
with an idea, an unclear business problem, an MVP, an existing application, a modernization
need, an AI opportunity, a cloud/platform problem, a reliability issue, or ongoing product
evolution.

## Typical engagement models

- **Discovery Sprint** — for ideas or problems needing clarity, scope and a roadmap.
- **Build Engagement** — for MVPs, new products, or substantial feature/platform delivery.
- **Modernization Engagement** — for existing systems requiring staged improvement.
- **Continuous Engineering** — for ongoing maintenance, support, reliability and product
  evolution.

## Cross-cutting capabilities

Three capabilities are applied across every engagement rather than sold as standalone services:
UX/UI, Quality Engineering, and Security by Design (see documents `30`–`32`).

## Product engineering proof

AROORAA brings product thinking from its own builds (MESA, Mindra, Smart Mirror, Arooraa Smart
Home) into client engagements — the same trade-offs, the same discipline.

## Starting a conversation

The two public entry points for a real engagement are the **Start a Project** flow (for anyone
with an idea, a business problem or an existing system that needs to work better) and **Contact**
(for general enquiries, partnerships, product questions, media, or careers-adjacent questions
that aren't a formal job application). See `41-project-engagement.md` and
`42-contact-and-support.md`.
