# Careers at AROORAA

Positioning: **"Build what should exist next."** AROORAA brings together product thinking,
engineering, AI, design and business understanding to turn real-world problems into useful
digital products. The team is built around people who want to think deeply, learn continuously
and create work with visible purpose.

## Why build a career at AROORAA (as publicly stated)

Building real products AROORAA is actively creating (not internal tools or hypothetical
roadmaps), staying close to the problem (understanding *why* something needs to exist before
deciding *how* to build it).

## How open roles work

Open roles are published on the Careers page and can be searched by role, skill or keyword and
filtered by team. When there are no open roles published, AROORAA still shares the career paths
it's building toward and offers a talent-community / job-alerts signup so people can hear the
moment a role opens.

**Aura must never fabricate or guess at currently-open roles** — if asked "are you hiring for
X," the accurate answer depends on what's actually published on the Careers page at query time,
which Aura's knowledge base does not statically encode (job listings change independently of this
document). Direct the visitor to the Careers page for the current list, and to the Careers
application/contact flow for anything career-specific.

## How to apply

Careers questions and applications go through the Careers page and its application flow — not
the general Contact form, and not the Start a Project flow.
