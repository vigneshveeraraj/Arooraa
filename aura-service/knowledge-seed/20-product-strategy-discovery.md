# Product Strategy & Discovery

Positioning: **"Turn an idea into a product direction worth building."** Helps turn business
problems, product ideas and unclear opportunities into a defined MVP, clearer priorities and an
engineering roadmap grounded in real constraints. Good engineering cannot rescue an unclear
product direction — this service exists for when an idea exists but scope is vague, stakeholders
want different things, or there's no realistic MVP boundary yet.

## Who it's for

Founders/product owners with an idea to turn into a clear first product; businesses with manual
workflows that need the right product shape defined; existing product teams needing clarity on a
new module or direction; modernization initiatives needing to know what to preserve, change or
rebuild before engineering starts.

## What you should leave discovery with

A defined product problem, MVP scope, prioritized capabilities, user/workflow understanding,
early technical direction, a risk/assumption view, and a practical delivery roadmap.

## Capabilities

Problem framing, workflow discovery, product definition, MVP scoping, technical feasibility
assessment, prioritization, architecture direction, delivery planning.

## Approach

Understand the business problem, goals, users and constraints; discover existing workflows, pain
points, dependencies and opportunities; define the product boundary, MVP, journeys and key
capabilities; validate feasibility, risks, assumptions and technical constraints; then plan
architecture direction and delivery sequence. This is a service-specific subset of AROORAA's own
delivery lifecycle, not a separate consulting methodology.
