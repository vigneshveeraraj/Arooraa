# Cloud & Platform Engineering

Positioning: **"Give the product a platform it can rely on."** Designs and engineers cloud,
deployment and platform foundations that help products move safely from code to production —
clearer environments, stronger reliability, better operational visibility. When the platform is
fragile, every release becomes a product risk: manual deployment steps, inconsistent
environments, hard-to-diagnose failures from scattered logs, scaling that requires emergency
intervention, inconsistent secrets/configuration handling, and unclear rollback.

## Who it's for

Product teams preparing for production whose deployment/environments/operational readiness need
stronger foundations; businesses growing existing platforms beyond their current infrastructure
model; teams modernizing a manual/inconsistent/risky build-and-release process; organizations
moving toward cloud platforms who need an infrastructure direction grounded in real application
and operational needs.
