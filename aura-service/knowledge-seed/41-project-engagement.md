# Starting a Project with AROORAA

Positioning: **"Tell us what should work better."** No complete specification is required —
start with the problem, whether that's a new idea, a recurring business problem, an existing
product that needs improvement, or a system that has become difficult to change. Philosophy:
**"Start with the problem. We'll help shape the solution."**

## What happens after someone submits a project enquiry

1. **You tell us the problem** — what you're trying to build, improve or solve.
2. **We review the context** — the product, business and engineering needs behind it.
3. **We contact you** — using the enquirer's preferred contact method.
4. **We shape the next step** — if there's a fit, AROORAA helps determine discovery, design,
   engineering or another suitable direction.

## The kinds of solutions a project can be about

A new product, an existing product, AI/data/automation, application modernization, a cloud/
platform need, a connected physical product, or "needs guidance" when the visitor isn't sure yet.

## The kinds of engagement a project can become

Discover & Define, Design & Build, Improve & Modernize, Add AI/Automation, Engineering
Collaboration, Continuous Product Partner, or "needs a recommendation" when it isn't clear yet.

## Aura's role in this flow

Aura can help a visitor think through their problem, explore relevant AROORAA capabilities, and
progressively clarify what they're trying to solve (see `93-aura-project-discovery-policy.md`
and `94-aura-handoff-policy.md`) — but only ever hands off an *explicitly visitor-approved*
summary into the existing Start a Project flow. Aura never submits a project enquiry on a
visitor's behalf without their clear consent, and never invents project details the visitor
didn't actually say.

## What Aura must not do here

Aura must never promise a specific response time, a specific price, or a guaranteed outcome —
none of these are established public facts, and inventing them would be a false commitment on
AROORAA's behalf.
