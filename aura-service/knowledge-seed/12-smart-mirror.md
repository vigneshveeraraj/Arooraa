# Smart Mirror

Smart Mirror is publicly labeled **"AROORAA PRODUCT · COMING SOON"** — an ambient-computing and
physical-product exploration of how personal intelligence, family coordination and connected-home
information can become part of an everyday object, built around Raspberry Pi, voice interaction
and edge/local intelligence. Positioning: **"The mirror that understands your day."**

**This is a concept-and-prototype-direction product, not a shipped one — never describe any
Smart Mirror capability as currently available or purchasable.**

## The problem it explores

Everyday information lives in too many places — a calendar in one app, reminders in another,
family information in a chat thread, smart-home devices in their own vendor apps, wellness data
elsewhere. Even connected, most smart homes remain too operationally complicated to feel
effortless. Smart Mirror explores computing that becomes part of the environment — personalized,
connected to the household, and quiet when it has nothing useful to say.

## What it can become (concept/prototype direction, not shipped)

- **Personal Intelligence** — a daily briefing and answered questions without reaching for a
  phone.
- **Family Coordination** — shared reminders, calendar context, household tasks.
- **Wellness** — glanceable wellness information and reminders, where supported.
- **Home Intelligence** — connected-home status, simple routines, energy awareness.
- **Household Memory** — household knowledge, notes and lists.
- **Security & Awareness** — selected home-security information and alerts, where supported.

Structured around **My Space** (personal) and **Family Space** (shared), the same pattern as
Mindra.

## Trust direction

Visible camera & microphone state, user-controlled privacy, private vs shared information,
guest/restricted experiences, local processing where appropriate, no always-on assumption.

## Engineering direction

Raspberry Pi 5 / edge platform, edge & local computing, a voice-interaction direction, a
React-based interface, local AI experimentation, connected-device integration, privacy-aware
processing, a hybrid edge/cloud direction.

## Status

Starting with the prototype direction: a polished mirror UI, daily information, personalization,
voice interaction, basic home integration and visible privacy controls. Richer family/shared
experiences, deeper household knowledge, selected energy/home intelligence, and closer
connections with Mindra come next. Richer ambient intelligence, validated wellness experiences
and commercial variants are long-term direction, built only once the home experience is proven —
none of this is available today.
