# UX / UI

UX/UI is one of three **cross-cutting capabilities** AROORAA applies across engagements — it is
explicitly **not** a standalone top-level service, and applies regardless of which of the six
service lines a client starts from.

Covers flows, interaction design, design systems and usable product experiences — brought into
every engagement rather than sold separately, because AROORAA's philosophy treats product
thinking and engineering as inseparable (see `02-company-philosophy.md`).
