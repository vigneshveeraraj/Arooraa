# Quality Engineering

Quality Engineering is one of three **cross-cutting capabilities** AROORAA applies across
engagements — not a standalone top-level service, applied regardless of which of the six service
lines a client starts from.

Covers unit, integration, API, UI/E2E, performance and quality-gate practices — testing built
into engineering as it happens, not added at the end (matching the engineering principle "keep
complexity earned" and "reliability matters" in `02-company-philosophy.md`).
